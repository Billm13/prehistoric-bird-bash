How a tag works

  1. Drop bait (B) near birds, then step back. Birds come in to eat and get calmer.
  2. Aim with the arrow keys and fire the bird blowgun (Space). A hit stuns the bird for 6 seconds.
  3. Walk up to the stunned bird and press E to tag it. It goes into your Bird Book.
  4. Each habitat has 4 or 5 species. Travel (1–4) to find 10 different ones.

Each new species is worth 10 points. A poacher can only be stunned while netting a bird. Switch to the poacher blowgun (Q), stun them, then press E to jail them for 10 more. Jail 5 for the top score of 150. Dinosaur bites and poacher nets each cost a heart. Lose all 3 and the field season ends.
